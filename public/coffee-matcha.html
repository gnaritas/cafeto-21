Prices

Smalls $3–$4, larges to $6.50

Most small sizes start at $3 or $4; larger sizes go up to $6.50. No upcharge mystery — the board at the counter is the truth, and these are the numbers the TNT Diner column published in June 2026.

Stay a while

Wi-Fi is free, the space is open and bright, and the shop has become a hub for students, remote workers and everyone between. Coffee in hand, crepe on the plate, eight hours a day, seven days a week.
