Creperia · Coffee · Downtown Tacoma

Cafeto 21

Said “caf-ay-to.”

A creperie with a coffee bar attached, on Broadway between 11th and 13th — sweet and savory crepes made to order, matcha and horchata lattes, waffles, toast and sandwiches. Open every day, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.

The crepes

Sweet and savory,
made to order

The Berries Delight piles strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, blackberries and Nutella into a fresh crepe. The Pesto goes savory with mozzarella and mixed greens. Crepes run $12.49 to $13.49.

The coffee bar

Espresso, matcha,
horchata lattes

All the basic coffee orders, plus matcha, horchata, smoothies and energy drinks. Most smalls start at $3 or $4; larger sizes go up to $6.50.

The counter

A Tacoma second,
built on a Renton original

Cafeto 21 is run by Esteban Regla and his cousin Juan Pablo — the pair behind Redondo Coffee & Crepes in Renton. Juan Pablo sold crepes off a street cart before their first shop.

Open every day

8 a.m. to 4 p.m., seven days a week

Morning coffee before work, a crepe between classes at the School of the Arts next door, an afternoon study session on the free Wi-Fi — the doors are open all week on Broadway, next to Tacoma Dance Studios and the Tacoma School of the Arts theater.
